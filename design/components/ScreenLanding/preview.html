How it works

From job post to hire in four steps

Built for HR teams who read hundreds of CVs and want to be fair to every one.

  1. 01

    Post a job

    Add the title, skills, experience, degree and an optional Apply by date.

  2. 02

    Share the link

    Candidates apply from any phone with a PDF CV. No account hunting.

  3. 03

    AI checks every CV

    Each CV gets a score out of 100, found and missing requirements, and the CV lines that prove them.

  4. 04

    You decide

    Shortlist, interview and hire in a simple pipeline. AI never decides for you.
