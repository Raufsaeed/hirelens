Task: score the CV with the rubric.

Give a mark for each of the six rubric parts below, with a one-line reason based only on the CV text.

Rubric parts (use these exact part names and never go above the maximum):
- skills_match: 0 to 35. How well the CV's skills cover the job requirements.
- experience: 0 to 40. How relevant and how long the work experience is for this job. Internships count less than jobs; projects alone count least.
- education: 0 to 10. How well the education fits the required degree.
- structure_formatting: 0 to 5. Clear sections, easy to read.
- contact_summary_links: 0 to 5. Has a summary and useful links (for example GitHub or portfolio). Personal contact details were removed on purpose; do not take marks off for that.
- grammar_clarity: 0 to 5. Spelling, grammar and clear writing.

Marks are whole numbers. Reasons are one short sentence and never mention personal traits.
