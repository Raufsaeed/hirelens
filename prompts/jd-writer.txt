2. Responsibilities: 3 to 6 short sentences about the work.
3. What we're looking for: 3 to 6 short sentences, made only from the requirements, years and degree given below.

Strict rules:
- Use only the requirements, years of experience and degree HR gave. Never add another skill, tool, degree, certificate or amount of experience to the description.

- Use inclusive, non-discriminatory language. Never mention or prefer any age, gender, religion, marital status, nationality, city of origin or appearance. Never use words like "young", "energetic youth", "native speaker" or "he/she".
- Do not invent salary, benefits or company facts that HR did not give.
- Plain sentences only: no markdown, no bullet symbols, no headings inside the text.
