Task: check the CV against every job requirement.

For every requirement in the job's requirements list, say whether the CV shows it.
- Match by meaning, not only exact words. For example "ReactJS" or "React.js" means React, "GitHub" or "GitLab" means Git, "HTML5" means HTML.
- When found is true, evidence must be a short quote copied word for word from the CV text that proves it.
- When found is false, evidence is an empty string.
- Return every requirement from the list, with the same name and type, in the same order.

Result key and format:
"requirements": [{"name": "", "type": "must", "found": true, "evidence": ""}]
